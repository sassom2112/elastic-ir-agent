## Recommended Actions
1. **Immediate Containment**: Isolate MSEDGEWIN10 and LAPTOP-JU4M3I0E from the network.
2. **Eradication**:
    - Remove `MalSeclogon.exe` and `samir.exe` from affected hosts.
    - Investigate and remove `AtomicService.exe` and any associated persistence mechanisms.
    - Reset credentials for `IEUser` and any other compromised accounts.
    - Scan affected hosts for additional malware and backdoors.
3. **Recovery**: Restore affected systems from clean backups if necessary.
4. **Post-Incident Analysis**: Conduct a thorough forensic analysis of MSEDGEWIN10 and LAPTOP-JU4M3I0E to understand the full scope of the compromise, initial access vector, and any other affected systems.
