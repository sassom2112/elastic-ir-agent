## Recommended Actions
1. **Immediate Containment**: Isolate MSEDGEWIN10 and LAPTOP-JU4M3I0E from the network. Reset passwords for IEUser and user01, and any other potentially compromised accounts.
2. **Eradication**: Remove `MalSeclogon.exe`, `samir.exe`, and `AtomicService.exe` from affected hosts. Review and remove any suspicious scheduled tasks, services, or registry modifications.
3. **Recovery**: Restore affected systems from known good backups if possible. Implement stronger access controls and multi-factor authentication for all user accounts. Conduct a thorough forensic analysis of MSEDGEWIN10 and LAPTOP-JU4M3I0E to identify the initial compromise vector and full extent of the breach.
