## Recommended Actions
1. **Immediate Containment**: Isolate `MSEDGEWIN10` and `LAPTOP-JU4M3I0E` from the network. Reset credentials for `IEUser` and `user01` across all systems.
2. **Eradication**: Remove `MalSeclogon.exe`, `samir.exe`, and `AtomicService.exe` from all affected hosts. Scan all affected hosts for additional malware and backdoors.
3. **Recovery**: Restore affected systems from known good backups if necessary. Implement stronger access controls and multi-factor authentication for all users. Review and harden system configurations to prevent similar attacks.
