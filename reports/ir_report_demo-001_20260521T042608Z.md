## Recommended Actions
1. **Immediate containment steps**:
    - Isolate MSEDGEWIN10 and LAPTOP-JU4M3I0E from the network.
    - Force password resets for `IEUser` and `user01` across all systems.
    - Disable or remove the `AtomicService` on MSEDGEWIN10.
2. **Eradication steps**:
    - Investigate and remove `MalSeclogon.exe` and `samir.exe` from MSEDGEWIN10 and LAPTOP-JU4M3I0E, respectively.
    - Perform a full antivirus/EDR scan on all affected hosts.
    - Review and revert any malicious registry modifications made by `IEUser` on MSEDGEWIN10.
3. **Recovery steps**:
    - Restore MSEDGEWIN10 and LAPTOP-JU4M3I0E from known good backups if available, or rebuild them.
    - Verify the integrity of system recovery features (VSS, Windows Backup, BCD settings).
    - Implement stronger access controls and multi-factor authentication for all user accounts.
    - Conduct a thorough review of security logs for any further indicators of compromise.
